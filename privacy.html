Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Austin's Assistant is a personal tool used only by its owner. It accesses the owner's own Google account data (such as Google Tasks) solely to show and update that data at the owner's request.

  • Data is processed on the owner's own computer and is not sold, shared or transferred to anyone else.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • No data is collected from anyone other than the owner.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
